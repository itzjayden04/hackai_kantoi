Read. Kantoi reads the text, whether you pasted it, shared it from WhatsApp, or sent a screenshot, PDF or voice note.

Protect your details. Names, phone, IC and account numbers are masked on the phone, before anything goes online.

Scam signs. Pressure words light up in English, Malay, Manglish, Chinese and Tamil, and Kantoi says which step of a known scam script this is.

Links. Every redirect is followed safely. Each hop shows domain age, certificate, hosting country, look-alike brands and login forms.

AI. Google Gemini writes a plain-English explanation. It only sees the masked text. Offline? Built-in rules still give a verdict.

Verdict. One clear answer, with every reason listed, so you can decide before you pay.
